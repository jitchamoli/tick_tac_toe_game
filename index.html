Decay Tic-Tac-Toe

Tic-tac-toe that can never end in a draw. Rules · Proof

Moves

    How it works

    • You may hold at most three marks.
    • From your fourth turn, your oldest mark is removed and the cell it sat on is burned — dead for both players — before you place.
    • Three in a row wins, at any moment.
    • If it is your turn and there is nowhere left to place, you lose.
